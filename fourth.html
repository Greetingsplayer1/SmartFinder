Elective and CTE Courses Overview

Audio and TV Production

Students learn how to produce audio, video, and broadcast-quality media by applying scripting, recording, editing, and production skills using industry-standard workflows.

Year 1: Student Choice

Year 2: DE TV and Media Production I

Year 3: DE TV and Media Production II

Year 4: DE TV and Media Production III or Communications Capstone Students Can Earn: Adobe Certified Professional Examinations Graphic Design and Digital Animation

Graphic Design and Digital Animation

Students build creative and technical skills in design and digital media by creating graphics, layouts, and animations using current software tools and industry-based project workflows.

Year 1: Student Choice

Year 2: Graphic Communications Systems (Graphic Design) or Digital Animation

Year 3: Graphic Communications Systems (Graphic Design) or Digital Animation

Year 4: Communications Capstone Students Can Earn: Adobe Certified Professional Examinations Courses for year 2 and 3 can be taken in either order.

Physical Therapy

This Program includes sports medicine technology and prepares students for multiple opportunities within growing health care fields.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: DE Physical Occupational Therapy I

Year 3: DE Medical Terminology or DE Physical Occupational Therapy II

Year 4: Physical Therapy Capstone Students Can Earn: National Health Science Assessment Physical Therapy Technician/ Aide Exam A.S. in Science

Emergency Medicine

This course helps students study how medical professionals respond to different emergency scenarios through hands-on learning, scenario-based decision making, and core emergency response concepts.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: DE Emergency Management I

Year 3: DE Emergency Management II or DE Medical Terminology

Year 4: EMT Capstone Students Can Earn: National Health Assessment EMT Certification A.S. in Science

Biomedical Technology

This course helps students apply scientific and engineering principles to biomedical lab work, investigations, and real-world medical technology topics in biology and chemistry.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: DE Medical Terminology

Year 3: DE Forensics & Biomedical Technology

Year 4: Health Science Capstone Students Can Earn National Health Science Certificate A.S. in Science

Veterinary Technology

This course explores education and careers in veterinary medicine and helps students build foundational animal-care and medical-support skills for working alongside veterinary professionals.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: Small Animal Care I & II

Year 3: DE Medical Terminology or Veterinary Science I & II

Year 4: Animal Care or Vet Assistant Capstone Students Can Earn National Health Science Assessment Certified Vet Assistant

Information Support & Services-Network Systems

Students learn how computer networks operate and how to protect them using core cybersecurity skills, troubleshooting, and secure-operations practices.

Year 1: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Year 2: DE Cybersecurity Operations & Computer Network Software Ops

Year 3: DE Cybersecurity Operations & Computer Network Software Ops Adv

Programming & Development

Students learn to design and build software and web projects by developing coding skills, problem-solving strategies, and real-world development practices.

Year 1: Webpage Design & Multimedia(DE Optional)

Year 2: DE Computer Programming

Year 3: DE Computer Programming Advanced

Year 4: DE Computer Programming Intensified

Database Management

Students learn how to design, build, and manage databases by working with data modeling, structured query concepts, and systems used to store and organize information.

Year 1: Database Design & Management (DE Optional)
