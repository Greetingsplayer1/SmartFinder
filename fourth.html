Elective and CTE Courses Overview

Audio and TV Production

Year 1: Student Choice

Year 2: DE TV and Media Production I

Year 3: DE TV and Media Production II

Year 4: DE TV and Media Production III or Communications Capstone Students Can Earn: Adobe Certified Professional Examinations Graphic Design and Digital Animation

Graphic Design and Digital Animation

Year 1: Student Choice

Year 2: Graphic Communications Systems (Graphic Design) or Digital Animation

Year 3: Graphic Communications Systems (Graphic Design) or Digital Animation

Year 4: Communications Capstone Students Can Earn: Adobe Certified Professional Examinations Courses for year 2 and 3 can be taken in either order.

Physical Therapy

This Program includes sports medicine technology and prepares students for multiple opportunities within growing health care fields.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: DE Physical Occupational Therapy I

Year 3: DE Medical Terminology or DE Physical Occupational Therapy II

Year 4: Physical Therapy Capstone Students Can Earn: National Health Science Assessment Physical Therapy Technician/ Aide Exam A.S. in Science

Emergency Medicine

This course provides students with the ability to study multiple medicines responses to different medical scenarios.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: DE Emergency Management I

Year 3: DE Emergency Management II or DE Medical Terminology

Year 4: EMT Capstone Students Can Earn: National Health Assessment EMT Certification A.S. in Science

Biomedical Technology

This course prepares students to know how to apply scientific principles to laboratory work and research in biology and chemistry.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: DE Medical Terminology

Year 3: DE Forensics & Biomedical Technology

Year 4: Health Science Capstone Students Can Earn National Health Science Certificate A.S. in Science

Veterinary Technology

This course explores education and careers in veterinary medicine and similar fields.

Year 1: Health Science

Year 2: Small Animal Care I & II

Year 3: DE Medical Terminology or Veterinary Science I & II

Year 4: Animal Care or Vet Assistant Capstone Students Can Earn National Health Science Assessment Certified Vet Assistant

Year 3: DE Cybersecurity Operations & Computer Network Software Ops Adv
