Core Classes Overview

Arlington tech offers a large number of classes and even with this large number there are still a few that are required for everyone to take. These classes inculde Mathmatics, History, Health and PE, Laguages, and Sciencez

Year 1: Algebra 1 (Sem1) and Geometry Principles (Sem 2)/Geometry (Normal or Intensified)/ Algebra 2

ALgebra 1: The focus is on an in depth understand of polynomial, rational, exponential, and functions in math. It can be taken for a full year of algebra or half a year followed by geometry.

Greometry: Geometry is faced pace course coving a wide range of topics forlm 3d shapes, triangle lines, and even some trig.

Algebra 2: It is an intensive math course made to connect the consepts from both algebra one and geometry, and in the class you cover advanced numbers and data analysis.

Year 1: World History/ World History Intensified

In world history you learn about topics ranging forom the earling eupoean civilizations up to modern time, in the class you do go over some heavy topics like WWII but the teachers always hep keep things intresting.

Year 1: Health and PE

health and PE is a required course for students to take through their years in high school and in it you learn about mental health, drug awarness, and of course PE. In PE you workout on wight machines play games like kickball, and more.

Year 1: English 9/ English 9 Intensified

Regular: It is a foundational, one-credit course focused on developing strong reading comprehension, writing, and analytical skills.

Intensified: This course is designed for the needs of the gifted and advanced students, and requires extensive reading and writing, intensive grammar and vocabulary, rigorous study of fiction, nonfiction, drama and poetry, and high-level performance in all strands of English language arts beyond what is required in English 9.
