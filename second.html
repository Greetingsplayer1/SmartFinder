Main Courses Overview

Mathematics

Students in Arlington Tech’s Mathematics pathway build a year-by-year sequence of algebra and geometry foundations, then progress into pre-calculus and calculus options (including DE offerings). The program is designed to prepare students for higher-level math by developing problem-solving skills, mathematical reasoning, and proficiency with college-level coursework.

Year 1: Algebra 1 (Sem1) and Geometry Principles (Sem 2)/Geometry (Normal or Intensified), requires algebra 1/ Algebra 2, requires algrebra 1 and geometry

Year 2: Algebra 2, requires algrebra 1 and geometry/ DE Pre-Calculas, requires algrebra 1, geometry, algebra 2

Year 3: Algebra 3/ DE Pre-Calculas/ DE Calc 1/ DE Statistics

Year 4: DE Calc 1, Calc 1/2, Calc 2/ DE Statistics/ DE Calc 3 Vector Analysis/ DE Linear Algebra

English

Students in Arlington Tech’s English pathway strengthen reading, writing, speaking, and critical-thinking skills through grade-level literature and composition. The sequence progresses from foundational coursework (English 9/10/11/12) into advanced options such as DE College Composition and DE World Literature, helping students communicate effectively and analyze texts at a college level.

Year 1: English 9, english 8 required/ English 9 Intensified, english 8 required

Year 2: English 10, english 9 required/ English 10 Intensified, english 9 required

Year 3: English 11/ DE College Composition

Year 4: English 12/ DE World Literature

Social Studies

Students in Arlington Tech’s Social Studies pathway study world and U.S. history while building an understanding of how government, economics, and civic life shape communities. The sequence includes world history (with an intensified option), economics and personal finance, and progression into U.S./Virginia history and U.S. government—supporting students with reasoning, document-based analysis, and responsible civic engagement.

Year 1: World History/ World History Intensified

Year 2: Economics and Personal Finance(Can take over the summer)

Year 3: US/VA History/ DE US/VA History

Year 4: US Government/ DE US Government

Language Courses

Students in Arlington Tech's World Language pathway develop communication skills, cultural competency, and proficiency in reading, writing, speaking, and listening. Courses follow a multi-year sequence from introductory through advanced/AP/DE options to prepare students for college-level work and global engagement.

Spanish

Year 1: Spanish 1 (Beginners).
Year 2: Spanish 2 — prerequisite: Spanish 1.
Year 3: Spanish 3 / Spanish 3 Honors / DE Spanish 3 — prerequisite: Spanish 2.
Year 4: AP Spanish Language & Culture, AP Spanish Literature, or DE Spanish (college-level options).

French

Year 3: French 3 / French 3 Honors / DE French 3 — prerequisite: French 2.
Year 4: AP French Language & Culture or DE French options.

Mandarin Chinese

Latin
