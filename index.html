Welcome to the Smart Finder project! This tool is designed to help students navigate their academic paths more effectively.

Our website gives an easy and navigable website for Arlington Tech students to see all the courses one can take at this high school. When someone applies to Arlington Tech, they need to know information about the classes available at this school. Having over 40 subjects, it may seem like a challenge to pick what one wants to do for the rest of that year. The Arlington Tech website does not give much support for that problem because it has information on many other topics. Our website focuses on the topic of the classes at Arlington Tech. This will give a website that will give tons of information while still being able to be searched with ease.
